        How it works

        Each dot is a player. Bigger dots mean more games played in the selected dates. A line joins two players who played each other; thicker lines mean more games together. Colours show the country, rank, or game statistic you choose under Colour by.

        Think of the lines as elastic bands: players who meet often pull closer together, while dots spread out to leave room. Groups form around shared opponents. Positions reflect these connections, and filters can change which players you see. Select a dot to see the player and their opponents. Everything shown comes from the loaded tournament games.
